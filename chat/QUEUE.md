Running the release layout in UIKit's animation instead of the keys' spring did not delay the resign. Next: find why UIKit completes our dismissal's placement at once (check `keyboardDismissPadding`, the keyboard layout guide's `usesBottomSafeArea`, and how the transcript's scroll view ends the pan).
