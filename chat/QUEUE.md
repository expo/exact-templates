- **The composer docks late after an interactive dismissal** (2026-10-27). Once it starts, its release follows the keyboard's curve frame for frame, but no frame is drawn from the finger's lift to about 92 ms. Time Profiler (xctrace, frames named by `dladdr` in a probed process, since the simulator's frameworks are only in its shared cache) shows the main thread busy for about 80 ms after the pan ends. Most of it is UIKit: `UIScrollToDismissSupport completedPlacementFrom:to:forController:` resigns the field (`UITextView resignFirstResponder`, then `UIKeyboardSceneDelegate setInputViews:`) about 5 ms after `scrollView:didFinishPanGesture:`. Exact's own refit and cover report under it take about 7 ms. UIKit can run that resign once the keyboard's dismissal animation completes instead, about 400 ms after the pan ends, leaving the first frame free. Running the release layout in UIKit's animation instead of the keys' spring did not delay the resign. Next: find why UIKit completes our dismissal's placement at once (check `keyboardDismissPadding`, the keyboard layout guide's `usesBottomSafeArea`, and how the transcript's scroll view ends the pan).
